Privacy Policy

iPortify — Assets and liabilities tracker

1 Overview

iPortify takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have.

✅ We do not sell your data · No ads in the app · We never collect bank account numbers, card details, or one-time passcodes

2 Data We Collect

Account information

  • The name you provide
  • Email address (only for email or Google sign-in)
  • A system-generated user identifier

Guest users provide no information; the system creates a temporary identifier only.

Financial data you enter

  • Names and values of assets you record
  • Names and balances of liabilities you record
  • Portfolio names and financial goals
  • Change history of your entries

You enter all of this data yourself. We do not connect to any bank or financial institution, and we do not retrieve data automatically.

Technical data

  • Crash reports
  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version in use

Usage data

We use Firebase Analytics to understand which features people use. The data sent is aggregated and non-identifying, for example:

  • Types of assets recorded, such as stocks or gold
  • Approximate item counts as ranges, such as 4-10 items
  • Selected language and theme
  • Sign-up method used

We never send monetary amounts, the names you assign to items, or any identifying information to our analytics system.

3 How We Use Your Data

DataPurpose
Account informationAuthentication and syncing across devices

We do not use your financial data for any purpose other than displaying it in the app, and we never disclose it to third parties for marketing.

4 Storage and Security

  • Data is stored on Google Firebase, which holds SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certifications
  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Data is isolated per account; other users cannot access yours
  • We use Firestore security rules to control access at the document level
  • You can enable biometric lock to prevent access by others using the same device

While we use appropriate safeguards, no system is completely secure. Please use a strong password and avoid reusing it across services.

6 Your Rights

  • Access — View all your data in the app at any time
  • Correct — Edit or delete any entry yourself
  • Export — Pro users can export data to Excel or CSV
  • Delete — Delete your account and all data from within the app
  • Withdraw consent — Stop using the service and uninstall at any time

How to delete your data

Go to Settings > Delete Account within the app. All data is permanently deleted immediately and cannot be recovered. See the Account Deletion page for details.

7 Data Retention

  • Account and financial data — retained while your account remains active
  • When you delete your account — removed immediately and permanently
  • Crash reports — retained for approximately 90 days per Crashlytics defaults
  • Non-identifying usage data — retained per Firebase Analytics defaults

8 Minors

This app is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with data, please contact us so we can remove it.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. For material changes, we will provide at least 7 days' notice through the app or by email.
